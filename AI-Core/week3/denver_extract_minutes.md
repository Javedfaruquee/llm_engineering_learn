# Denver City Council Meeting Minutes - October 9, 2023

## Attendees
- 11 members present: Councilman Lopez, Madam Secretary, Black Clark, Espinosa, Flynn, Gilmore, Cashman, Kenich, New, Ortega, Sussman

## Location
- Denver City Council Chambers

## Date
- October 9, 2023

## Summary
The Denver City Council meeting focused on the upcoming Confluence Week, the significance of Indigenous Peoples Day, and various community events. Councilman Lopez read a proclamation recognizing the contributions of Indigenous peoples to Denver and Colorado. The council discussed the importance of inclusivity and respect for all cultures, especially in light of current divisive events. The meeting also included announcements for the first-ever Halloween parade on Broadway and the second annual Indigenous Peoples Day.

## Discussion Points
- The creation and symbolism behind the new city logo, which features water to represent the importance of water issues globally.
- The celebration of Confluence Week, highlighting Indigenous events and fostering community togetherness.
- The observance of Indigenous Peoples Day, recognizing the contributions of Indigenous peoples to the city's history and culture.
- The importance of inclusivity and respect for all cultures, particularly in the context of current divisive events.

## Takeaways
- The city logo's design reflects the importance of water and the confluence of two rivers, symbolizing unity and the merging of cultures.
- Confluence Week aims to bring the community together and celebrate Indigenous events.
- Indigenous Peoples Day is officially recognized, with a proclamation issued to honor the cultural and foundational contributions of Indigenous peoples.
- The council emphasizes the importance of inclusivity and respect for all cultures, especially during challenging times.

## Action Items
- Councilman Lopez: Read and discuss the proclamation recognizing Indigenous Peoples Day.
- Council members: Support the proclamation and continue to promote inclusivity and respect for all cultures.
- Madam Secretary: Ensure the proclamation is attested and sealed, and transmit a copy to the Denver American Indian Commission, Denver City and County School District Number One, and the Colorado Commission on Indian Affairs.

## Additional Announcements
- The first-ever Halloween parade on Broadway and Lucky District 7 is scheduled for October 21st at 6 p.m. Councilman Clark invited everyone to join the family-friendly event.
- Councilman Lopez expressed pride in the city's growth and the contributions of Indigenous peoples, emphasizing that pride in one's culture does not require contempt or disrespect of another. He also highlighted the importance of addressing critical issues facing the community, such as poverty, lack of access to services, and housing.
