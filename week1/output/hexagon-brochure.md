# Hexagon: Measuring the World, Shaping the Future

---

## Who We Are

Hexagon is a global leader in precision measurement technologies, positioning, and autonomous solutions. We empower vital industries—ranging from manufacturing and construction to aerospace, agriculture, and mining—to build, navigate, and innovate confidently. Driven by the mission to define the future through measurement, Hexagon's advanced technologies cover scales from microns to Mars.

Our purpose is to drive productivity, quality, safety, and sustainability where it matters most. Operating at the intersection of sensors, digital twins, spatial intelligence, robotics, and AI, we enable smarter, safer, and more autonomous industrial processes worldwide.

At Hexagon, our culture centers on being:
